## Long description (use verbatim)

Verity is a real-time social media and prediction market integrity layer for traders, funds, and quant desks. It scans Reddit, X, YouTube, and 50,000+ public news sources every 15 minutes, classifies the signal, and surfaces coordinated activity, narrative inconsistencies, and source conflicts before they move the price.

Through this MCP server, your AI workflow can call Verity directly. Six tools:

- **verity-score** - composite integrity score for a ticker or topic, with a contributing-factor breakdown.
- **verity-scan** - real-time scan returning a structured pre-trade verdict.
- **morning-brief** - daily briefing of overnight signals across a watchlist.
- **cross-check-alert** - cross-reference a claim against authoritative sources; returns matched citations, conflicts, and a confidence verdict for compliance trails.
- **disinfo-alert** - flag coordinated narrative patterns around a topic or ticker.
- **coordination-heat** - heat score for a topic, ticker, or narrative across public social platforms.

Pricing follows the verityskills.com tier model. Seven-day trial gives Fund-equivalent access without a credit card; ten invocations cap the trial. Paid tiers start at $49 a month and unlock full output on every tool.

Verity is not financial advice. It is the manipulation check your AI workflow runs before any significant position. The trade does not fire until Verity clears it.
