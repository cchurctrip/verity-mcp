Pulled from `marketing-skills/verity-positioning-angles-feb2026.md` (Angle 8: Skills-Era line + Angle 2: Stop being exit liquidity headline). Brand rule: tagline goes into every marketplace listing verbatim. No paraphrasing.

## Primary tagline (use this everywhere)

> The manipulation check your stack was missing. Your AI workflow calls Verity before any significant position. The trade does not fire until Verity clears it.

## Short variant (when 80-char limit)

> Stop being exit liquidity. Pre-trade manipulation check for your AI workflow.

## Long variant (when listing accepts 200 chars)

> Verity gives your AI workflow a structured manipulation-and-disinformation check before any significant position. Six callable tools cover scoring, scanning, cross-checks, and morning briefs. Trial free for seven days.

## What NOT to say

- Do not name specific platforms beyond what the live site names (Reddit, X, YouTube, financial news, prediction markets are the allowed surface; everything else is "social media" or "public sources").
- Do not name LLM-vendor SDKs or detection-script identifiers.
- Do not promise predictions or financial advice. Verity is an integrity / monitoring tool, not a recommendation engine.
- Do not name government or intelligence customers; Verity has none and the comparison is misleading.
